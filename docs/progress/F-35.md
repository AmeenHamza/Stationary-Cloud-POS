# F-35 — Archive views for old bills and khata entries

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phases 8–9 · Rule: D-12 (archive instead of bulk delete)

## What was built

- Order history and both ledgers show the last 90 days by default. "Older than 90 days too" sends
  `includeArchived` and pages through everything.
- Order history also accepts a business-date range (`from`, `to`), which replaces the window.
- Nothing is deleted: the old app's "delete before date" and "delete old history" are replaced, as D-12
  decided.
- The window is measured on the server: `created_at` against the API's clock for orders, and the ledger's
  `created_at` for khata.

## Tests

- `khata-vendors-ledger.int.spec.ts` (PAG-08): 60 June entries are hidden by default. With
  `includeArchived` there are 61 rows in pages of 50 and 11, with no duplicates.
- `sales-refunds.int.spec.ts` (F-15, F-35):
  - newest-first order list and search by bill-number prefix;
  - a bill back-dated to June is hidden by default and listed with `includeArchived`.

# F-36 — Migration of the old system's data

**Status:** COMPLETE: migrated into production on 2026-11-10, validated (36/36) ·
Rules: D-21, A-07, Q27, Q35, Q41–Q44, ADR-048 · Tool: [tools/legacy-migration](../../tools/legacy-migration/README.md)

- **Analysis (Phase 1):** the old database was read in read-only sessions: 11 tables, about 5,500 rows,
  11 MB. The mapping, the data findings and a validation baseline are in
  [MAPPING.md](../../tools/legacy-migration/MAPPING.md). No personal data was printed or copied.
- **Engine:**
  - `check` compares the schema.
  - `dry-run` writes nothing and prints a fingerprint.
  - `apply --confirm <fingerprint>` writes and validates in one transaction, then commits or rolls back.
  - `verify` compares the two databases at any time.
- **Owner decisions:** loyalty points are not migrated (Q41), 4 payments with no method become cash (Q42),
  refunds take their bill's date (Q43), and the new GST setting is kept (Q44).
- **Not migrated:** FBR numbers (D-03), loyalty points, old logins (A-07), the Groq key, and one discount
  reason (MAPPING.md §7).

## Limitations

- **Bills before 30 October 2026** were deleted in the old app before the migration existed. Their khata
  entries are migrated as manual Udhaar, so balances are right, but the bills cannot be reprinted.
- **Refund dates** were never recorded by the old app; each refund is dated on its bill (Q43). Reports for
  30 October – 9 November show refunds on those days.
- **Merging into a database in use is refused.** The new database must hold only migrated data (or nothing).
- **No Neon branch was made before the production run** (the tool has no access to the Neon console). The
  database held only logins and settings then; Neon's restore to a time before 02:12 Pakistan time on
  2026-11-10 is the way back.
- **Data entered in the old system after the run does not come across.** The owner stopped billing there first.

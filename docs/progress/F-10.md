# F-10 — Restock

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 7 · Rules: BR-01 (stock in pieces), D-16

## What was built

- `restockProduct(input: { idempotencyKey, productId, boxes, pieces, note })` [K] (`inventory:restock`,
  admins). It locks the active product row, adds `boxes × piecesPerBox + pieces` (domain `restockPieces`) in
  one statement, writes a `RESTOCK` movement with `balance_after`, and emits `stock.changed`, plus
  `lowStock.countChanged` when the product crosses its threshold. It audits `stock.restocked`. Archived
  products are refused (`NOT_FOUND`).
- Web: Restock dialog with a boxes field (only when pieces per box > 1), loose pieces, a note, and a live
  preview "Adding N pcs · New total M pcs" (FEATURES.md §3.3).

# F-39 — A discount on specific items of a bill

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-11-08) · Owner update · Rules: D-27, Q39, BR-25, ADR-045

- **At the counter:** the overall discount in the Payment dialog is unchanged. Below it, "Discount on specific
  items" opens one row per cart line, each with No Discount, Flat (Rs) or Percentage (%). It stays folded until
  needed.
- **The maths** lives only in `@pos/domain`, which the API and the cart preview both run:
  - A flat amount comes off the whole line, and a percentage is of the line.
  - Item discounts come off first; the overall discount applies to what is left; taxes come after both.
  - Example: Rs 1,000 with 10 % off + Rs 500, then 10 % overall: 100, then 140 of the remaining 1,400, total
    Rs 1,260.
- **Storage:** `line_discount` still holds everything taken off a line, so the totals, reports and refund maths
  work unchanged, and a refund pays back the discounted price. `item_discount` and `item_discount_total` record
  the item part. Migration `20261108100000_item_discounts`.
- **Where it shows:** payment totals, order history ("−Rs 90.00 (10% + items)") and the receipt (under the item,
  and in the totals).
- **Permission:** any non-`NONE` discount, overall or on an item, needs `sales:discount` (D-11).
- **Draft safety:** a half-typed item discount survives a reload and never costs the cashier the line.

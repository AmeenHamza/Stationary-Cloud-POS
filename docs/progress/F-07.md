# F-07 — Dashboard charts and summaries

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 10 · Rules: Q18, Q28, Q30, Q31 (payment methods = current month), Q36
(least selling = products sold in the period)

## What was built

- **Sales analytics:**
  - Net sales by month for a chosen year: this year in the accent colour, the year before in gray context.
  - The year's total, and this month against the same month a year earlier.
- **Last 7 days:** net sales per day, all products or one product picked through a server search (10
  matches; never the full catalogue).
- **Products, last 30 days:** highest and least selling by pieces (sales minus returns), with amounts.
- **Discount summary** (today, this week, this month), **GST summary** (today, this month, average per bill),
  and **payment methods this month** as ranked bars.
- **Charts** are plain HTML/CSS in `components/charts`, loaded as a separate chunk after the tiles (ADR-039):
  - Bars ≤ 24 px with rounded tops and one axis.
  - The column hover tooltip lists every series and also opens on keyboard focus; columns have a
    screen-reader table.
  - Payment methods print their values at the bar tips, so they need no tooltip.
  - Colours were validated for contrast and colour blindness against both card surfaces.
- `DashboardHistory` is read once (closed periods) and on "Refresh".

## Limitations

- The weekly chart's product filter shows up to 10 search matches; a more specific search narrows it.
- Charts were checked in the e2e run and by unit tests, not yet by the owner on a counter monitor.

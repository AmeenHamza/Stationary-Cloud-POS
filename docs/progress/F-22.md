# F-22 — Six payment methods including Udhaar (and the billing batch)

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 9 · Rules: D-15 (Udhaar is full credit to one customer), D-05,
D-11, rule 6 (financial correctness). This report also holds the verification for Phases 8–9 (batches 3 + 4).

## What was built

- **Payment dialog** (ADR-038):
  - Totals, discount (F-20), Bill GST (F-21), then the six methods: Cash, Card, Easypaisa, JazzCash, Bank
    transfer, Udhaar (credit).
  - **Udhaar** opens `UdhaarCustomerPicker`: server search, 50 per page, and quick-add. "Process Payment" is
    disabled until a customer is chosen.
  - "Process Payment · Rs X" is disabled while offline (F-37) and while an earlier sale is unconfirmed.
- **`createSale` [K]** (SYSTEM_DESIGN §3.3):
  - Products are locked in ascending id order and priced with `calculateBill` from the locked rows.
  - A different total than the one shown is refused with `PRICE_CHANGED` and the server quote; the terminal
    re-reads its products and shows the new total.
  - The bill number comes from the DB business date. Order and lines are written, and stock moves in one
    statement for the whole bill.
  - Udhaar posts `CREDIT_SALE` on the khata in the same transaction.
  - Outbox: `stock.changed`, `order.created` and `customer.balanceChanged`.
  - Above-stock sales are allowed and reported, unless the product is already at 0 or below (D-16). A retry with the same key replays the bill.
- **`saleStatus(idempotencyKey)`** and the **pending-sale flow** (SYSTEM_DESIGN §3.4):
  - The input and key are saved before sending.
  - Network failures or a 30 s timeout re-send the same key (up to 5, jittered, waiting for `online`).
  - A reload asks `saleStatus` once; the banner offers Check status, Retry sale (same key) or Discard.
- **Receipt** (F-23), **order history** (F-15), **refunds** (F-16) and **terminal modals** (F-28) have their
  own reports.

## Found and fixed during the batch

1. **DB transfer per bill was over budget.** A 3-line scan-based bill moved 29.5 KB against a 25 KB budget
   (NET-03, found by the new test).
   - Cause: stock changed one product at a time, with an UPDATE, a re-read with the category, and an INSERT
     per line (12 statements for 3 lines).
   - Fix: one UPDATE (`unnest`) plus one `createMany` per bill; refunds take their row locks in ascending
     order first.
   - Result: the sale is 13.6 KB in 17 statements, and the whole bill about 18.8 KB.
2. **Cart lines carried a stray field.** Adding a product stored the action's `type` field on the cart line.
   Found by the reducer test and fixed.
3. **Three billing lists were missing from the pagination allow-list.** `OrderDetail.lines`,
   `Receipt.lines` and `CreateSalePayload.stockWarnings` are bounded by `MAX_LINES_PER_SALE`; they are now
   allow-listed with that bound (`scripts/pagination-allowlist.json`).
4. **The sales migration was over the size limit.** It was 214 lines (rule 3) and is now 176: blank lines
   between Prisma's generated statements were removed, and the SQL is unchanged.
   - The local dev database's recorded checksum for that migration was updated to match. This is one row of
     metadata; no data was touched, and `prisma migrate status` reports "up to date".
5. **Duplicated maths was moved into `@pos/domain`.**
   - `billDiscountOf` / `billGstOf` parse discount and GST entries.
   - `refundSnapshotOf` turns a stored bill into refund input.
   - The API and the browser now share both instead of keeping two copies.
6. **The e2e suite hit the per-IP login limit.** With the new billing specs, the suite signed in more than
   30 times from 127.0.0.1 within 15 minutes (`login-ip`, API.md §8), and the last spec's sign-in was
   refused.
   - The production limit is unchanged.
   - Specs that are not about logging in now use `signInOnce` (one form sign-in per user per run, then the
     same session cookie). The auth and staff specs still sign in through the form every time.
7. **Billing stayed paused for up to 30 s after the network returned** when a request had failed while
   offline: nothing retried HTTP until the socket's back-off. The `online` event now runs the recovery
   refetch at once (SYSTEM_DESIGN §5.8, [F-37](F-37.md)).

## Limitations

- **Real printer:** see [F-23](F-23.md#limitations); the 80 mm CSS has not been tried on the counter printer.
- **Integration start-up timeouts.** On this PC, a harness occasionally fails to start under load with an
  ioredis "Command timed out" (200 ms or 1 s app command timeouts), before any test of that file runs.
  - It was first seen in Phase 7 (F-08 limitations). This batch it hit `catalog-lists`, `sessions`,
    `sales-billing` and `sales-resilience` once each in mixed runs.
  - Every one of them passes when re-run, and no assertion has ever failed this way.
  - **Resolved 2026-11-01:**
    - The command was ioredis's own ready check (`INFO`), which can exceed the cache client's 200 ms
      timeout on a loaded machine.
    - The harness waited with `events.once`, which rejects on that transient `error` even though the
      client reconnects.
    - `waitForStatus` now waits for the status event only (see [F-11](F-11.md#verification)).
- **Not done here:** RT-03 and RT-04 with real sales across two API instances (the mechanism is tested at
  the Pub/Sub level since Phase 4; Phase 14 has the two-instance WebSocket test).
- **Phase 14 measurement items** (NETWORK_OPTIMIZATION.md §9): each scan reads the category name in a second
  statement, and `createSale` re-reads the order after inserting it. Both are within budget today.

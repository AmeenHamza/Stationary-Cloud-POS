# F-16 — Full and partial refunds

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 9 · Rules: D-11 (cashiers refund), D-14 (always restock), A-03
(Udhaar refunds credit the khata), A-06 (refund maths), Q23 (statuses)

- **API:** `refundOrder` [K] runs as one transaction:
  - Lock the bill, then validate quantities (`REFUND_EXCEEDS_REMAINING`).
  - Price with `@pos/domain` `calculateRefund`, and refuse an amount other than the one shown
    (`QUOTE_MISMATCH` with the server amount).
  - Record the refund, its lines and the line and order totals, with status `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` or
    `REFUNDED`.
  - Restock in ascending product order. For an Udhaar bill, add a `REFUND_CREDIT` on the khata.
  - Write `order.refunded`, `stock.changed` and the `sale.refunded` audit.
- **Web refund dialog** (`#17`):
  - Net amount and already-refunded total.
  - Per line: Sold, Refunded, Remaining and a Refund qty stepper; Select all remaining; Clear; "N unit(s)
    selected across M item line(s)".
  - Reason, then Refund Selected or Refund All with amounts priced by the same domain function.
  - A changed bill (another counter refunded) refetches and asks again.
  - A retry of the same request keeps its key.

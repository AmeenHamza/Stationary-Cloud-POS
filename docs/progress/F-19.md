# F-19 — Cart

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 9 · Rules: Q34 (≤ 100 lines, ≤ 10,000 per line), SYSTEM_DESIGN §3.1

## What was built

- **`CartProvider`** in the terminal layout holds lines `{ productId, unit, quantity }` (no prices), the
  discount, Bill GST, payment method and the Udhaar customer.
- **Saving:** the cart is saved per user (`pos:cart:{userId}`) on every change and restored on reload; a
  saved cart is re-validated with the API's line schema.
- **Pricing:** the preview reads cached products. They are fetched by id (≤ 50 per request) only when the
  cache lacks them, through a `productsByIds` cache policy.
- **Cart panel:** name, Piece/Box, − quantity +, unit price, line total, remove, item count, subtotal,
  Clear, Proceed to Payment; "Tap a product to add it" when empty.
- **When the cart is cleared:** only after a confirmed sale.

# F-23 — Receipt printing (80 mm)

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 9 · Rules: D-13, Q6b default content

- **`components/receipt`:** `ReceiptBody` prints the shop name, address, phone, bill number, date and time,
  cashier, each line (qty × unit price, line total), subtotal, discount, product tax, Bill GST with its
  rate, TOTAL and payment method. Udhaar bills add the customer and their new khata balance, then the
  footer.
- **Printing:** `ReceiptPrint` renders into a print-only portal (`styles/print-receipt.css`: `@page 80mm`,
  72 mm content) and calls `window.print()` once fonts are ready.
- **When it prints:**
  - A new sale prints at once.
  - A sale confirmed after a reload prints on request.
  - Order history can reprint (`orderReceipt`).
  - Counter PCs print silently with Chrome `--kiosk-printing` (DEPLOYMENT.md §9).
- **Tests:**
  - E2E-02: print invoked once after the sale (`window.print` stubbed).
  - E2E-04: reprint from the admin order history.
  - `sales-budgets.int.spec.ts`: the sale response with its receipt is ≤ 6 KB.
  - All PASS.

## Limitations

- Printing on a real 80 mm thermal printer has not been checked. The CSS targets 80 mm paper, and the owner
  should print one test receipt on the counter printer before go-live.

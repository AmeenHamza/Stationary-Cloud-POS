# F-40 — Regenerate a customer's bill from the khata

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-11-08) · Owner update · Rules: Q40 · Builds on F-23, F-25

- **In the khata:** each credit-sale row now names its bill and has a "Regenerate bill" button, on the admin
  page and in the terminal's Khata modal. Cashiers can use it, like any reprint.
- **What prints:** the 80 mm bill rebuilt from what was stored (`orderReceipt`), so a later price change does
  not alter it, and the balance shown is the one right after that bill.
- **Also fixed:** the printed receipt never showed the customer, although SYSTEM_DESIGN.md §3.5 says Udhaar bills
  do. It now prints "Customer" and "Balance after this bill" on Udhaar bills, at the counter as well.

## Limitations

- **Only Udhaar bills** are linked to a customer. A cash, card or wallet sale is not linked to anyone at the
  counter, so it cannot appear in a khata; reprint those from order history by bill number (Q40).
- **Entries older than 90 days** need the existing "Show entries older than 90 days" toggle (F-35).
- **A regenerated bill does not show later refunds**, because it reprints the original charge.

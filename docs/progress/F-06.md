# F-06 — Dashboard tiles

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 10 · Rules: Q18 (Asia/Karachi days, weeks from Monday), Q28 (refunds
on their own date), Q30 (GST = product tax + Bill GST), D-23 (no expiry tile)

## What was built

- **API:** `dashboard(date)` (admins, `modules/reporting`, ADR-039). Every field is one SQL aggregate, run only
  when selected:
  - `today`: net sales, bills not fully refunded, discount, tax and refunds.
  - `inventory`: selling and cost value of positive stock, and the active product count.
  - `customerDues`: the sum of positive customer balances.
  - `discountSummary` and `taxSummary`, which share one statement.
  - `paymentMethods(period)`.
- **Web `/dashboard` ("Overview", the admin landing page):**
  - Tiles: today's sales, today's orders, total products, low stock (the existing `lowStockCount` query),
    customer dues, and inventory selling and cost value.
  - `DashboardLive` (tiles and summaries) is refetched, coalesced, while the screen is open, after
    `order.created`, `order.refunded`, `stock.changed` and `customer.balanceChanged` (no polling).

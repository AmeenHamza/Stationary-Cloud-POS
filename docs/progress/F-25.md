# F-25 — Customer khata

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phase 8 · Rules: D-11 (cashiers keep khata), D-12 (archive), D-15,
Q25 (balance wording), Q33 (archive needs a zero balance), A-03 (refund credit)

## What was built

- **API (`modules/khata`, ADR-037):**
  - `customers(filter: { search, withBalanceOnly, includeArchived })` (search: name or phone digits) and
    `customer(id)`.
  - `customerLedger(customerId, includeArchived)`: newest first, keyset pages of 50, the last 90 days unless
    asked (F-35).
  - `createCustomer` / `updateCustomer` [V] and `archiveCustomer` / `restoreCustomer` (admins only;
    `BALANCE_NOT_ZERO` unless settled).
  - `addCustomerCredit` ("Add Udhaar", `MANUAL_CREDIT`) and `receiveCustomerPayment` (`PAYMENT`, any method
    except Udhaar), both [K].
  - Entry mutations return the customer with the new balance.
- **One posting engine for both khatas** (`ledger/ledger-posting.service.ts`):
  - `balance = balance + delta` in one statement under the row lock.
  - The entry with that `balance_after`.
  - `customer.balanceChanged` (ids and two-decimal money only, no names or phones) and the audit row.
  - Sales join the same transaction for `CREDIT_SALE` (F-22) and `REFUND_CREDIT` (F-16).
- **Database:** `khata.prisma` and migration `20261030210411_khata`:
  - CHECKs on the sign of each entry type.
  - Method only on `PAYMENT`, never `UDHAAR`.
  - Sale and refund references with one credit per bill and one per refund.
  - Trigram search on name and partial indexes on phone digits.
- **Web:**
  - Admin `/customers` (search, with-balance and archived filters, add, open) and `/customers/[id]`
    (balance header, entries with the 90-day toggle, Add Udhaar, Receive payment, edit, archive or restore).
  - Shared `components/ledger/*` serve customers and vendors.
  - The live `balanceChanged` patch, and a refetch of an open ledger.

## Limitations

- On a later ledger page a balance event refetches that page; SYSTEM_DESIGN §5.9 suggested a "new entries"
  banner there. Keyset pages do not shift, so the refetch is harmless. API.md §7.2 records the behaviour.
- Credit-sale ledger rows show "Udhaar bill" with the bill's `orderId`; the bill number itself is not shown on
  the row yet.

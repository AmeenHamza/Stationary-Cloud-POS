# F-13 — Low stock list and badge

**Status:** COMPLETE (2026-10-31) · Phases 7, 11 · Rule: BR-02 (low when stock ≤ threshold). The list and the
live badge shipped in Phase 7; the CSV and PDF export shipped with F-11 (Phase 11).

## What was built

- `lowStockProducts(first, after)`: active products with `stock_pieces <= low_stock_threshold_pieces`, sorted
  `STOCK_ASC` (emptiest first), keyset pages of 50, served by the partial index `products_low_stock_idx`.
- `lowStockCount`: one COUNT over the same partial index.
- `lowStock.countChanged` (admins) when a product crosses its threshold: create, update, restock, adjust,
  archive, restore.
- Web: Low stock screen (same table and actions as products; Restock from the row) and the sidebar badge on
  every admin page. Both refetch only on the event (coalesced), never on a timer.
